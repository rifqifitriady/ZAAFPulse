# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want ZAAFPulse to be a project where anyone can ask, learn and contribute, whatever their experience, background or the tools they use. A first question, a beginner's pull request or a non-native English sentence should never feel risky here.

## Expected behaviour

- Be patient and kind, especially with people who are new to the project or to open source.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific about what to change and why.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings.
- Respect other people's time, privacy and data. Never paste someone else's logs, keys or private information.
- Accept that maintainers make the final call on scope and direction, even when you disagree.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Sexual attention or content.
- Threats.
- Publishing others' private information (including credentials, transcripts or notes) without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

- **Non-sensitive matters:** open an issue.
- **Sensitive matters:** contact the maintainer, [@rifqifitriady](https://github.com/rifqifitriady), privately. If you are unsure, use the repository's private reporting channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Reports are handled confidentially, and reporters are protected from retaliation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may:

- edit, hide or delete comments, commits, code and issues that violate this document;
- warn contributors, or block them temporarily or permanently.
